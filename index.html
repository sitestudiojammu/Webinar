WebinarCast

Broadcast your screen, camera and voice to a live audience. Attendees register with their name and mobile number, watch live, and message you privately.

Runs peer-to-peer over WebRTC. Best for small to medium audiences (up to about 15–25 attendees, since your device sends the stream to each one). Host this file on any HTTPS site so the link works on every device.
